Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Conditional Formatting ("the App," "we," "us") handles information when you use our monday.com application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

2. Information We Access

The App accesses the following data through the monday.com platform API:

  • Board data: Column names, column types, item names, and cell values (status labels, numbers, dates, text) — solely to evaluate your formatting rules and render colored cells.
  • User identity: Your monday.com user ID and account ID — used to store your formatting rules and check your subscription plan tier.

3. Information We Store

All formatting rules you create are stored using monday.com's built-in Storage API (monday.storage), which keeps data within the monday.com platform. We do not operate any external servers or databases.

We do not store:

  • Your board data, item values, or column contents
  • Personal information such as names, emails, or contact details
  • Authentication tokens or credentials
  • Usage analytics, browsing history, or behavioral data

4. Information We Share

We do not share, sell, rent, or transmit any data to third parties. The App makes no external API calls and does not connect to any services outside of the monday.com platform.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, advertising, or data processing services. No cookies are set by the App.

6. Data Security

Because the App operates entirely within the monday.com platform and does not transmit data externally, your data benefits from monday.com's enterprise-grade security infrastructure. The App itself is a client-side application hosted on monday.com's CDN with no server-side component.

7. Data Retention

Formatting rules persist in monday.com's storage as long as the App is installed on your account. When you uninstall the App, stored rules are removed according to monday.com's standard data retention policies.

8. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete any formatting rule at any time through the App interface
  • Uninstall the App to remove all stored data
  • Contact us with any privacy-related questions

9. Children's Privacy

The App is a business tool provided through the monday.com platform and is not intended for use by children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through the monday.com marketplace listing or at the support email listed on our app page.
