Frequently asked questions

How does billing work?

All subscriptions are managed through monday.com's built-in billing system. You subscribe and manage your plan directly from the monday.com platform — no separate account needed.

Can I try paid features before subscribing?

The Free plan lets you experience the core formatting engine with up to 3 rules. Upgrade anytime when you need unlimited rules, row highlighting, or cross-column formatting.

Can I downgrade or cancel?

Yes. You can change or cancel your plan at any time through monday.com. If you downgrade, your existing rules are preserved but rules beyond the plan limit will be paused.

Is there a per-board charge?

No. Your plan covers all boards in your account. Add Conditional Formatting to as many boards as you like.
